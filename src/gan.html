Challenges of Testing GaN Devices

High Voltage and High Current Characterization

Power GaN devices frequently operate at elevated voltages and high peak currents. Test systems must safely source and measure these conditions while maintaining measurement accuracy and repeatability.

Fast Switching Dynamics

GaN devices switch significantly faster than conventional silicon technologies, requiring precise measurement of switching events, transient behavior, dv/dt, and di/dt characteristics.

Low Current and Leakage Measurements

The wide-bandgap properties of GaN can enable extremely low off-state currents, creating a need for sensitive measurement instrumentation capable of resolving very small leakage currents while also supporting high-voltage test conditions.

Dynamic Device Behavior

Accurate characterization increasingly requires evaluation of dynamic RDS(on), switching energy losses, thermal effects, parasitic interactions, and transient operating conditions.

Manufacturing and Reliability Challenges

Maintaining high-quality epitaxial GaN layers on silicon substrates remains an important manufacturing challenge. Material and thermal expansion mismatches can introduce defects that impact performance, yield, and reliability.
